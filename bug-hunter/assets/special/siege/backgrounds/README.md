# Siege Cartoon backgrounds (by world)

The owner's panoramic battle backgrounds, kept by world for the per-world/per-stage scenes. The heroes' land is clean on the left and the Darkness invades on the right. Most are about 2172 × 724. Not loaded by the game yet: the battle still uses `../bg-*.webp`.

- `world-1/` green kingdom: castle and lake valley, king's walls, stone bridge over waterfalls, windmill village, mossy arches.
- `world-2/` sky islands: temple, cannon citadel, the five sky-island landscapes without ground (`scene-3..7`), chained islands, temple under the vortex.
- `world-3/` forest and swamp: rune forest, swamp lake, the corrupted giant tree (two), lakeside woodcutter's cabin.
- `world-4/` desert: ruins and the dark pyramid portal, sandstorm, ruined arches, giant beast bones.
- `world-5/` snow: ice castle, glacier lake, pine valley, castle under the vortex, frozen crystal lake.
- `world-6/` the Darkness: lava castle, lava falls under red lightning, ruins under the vortex, burnt forest.
- `grounds/` transparent ground strips (a stone path over a mossy rock wall, some turning corrupt to the right), for a separate ground layer.

Scenes with their own ground have the path's top at about 69–76% of the height. The game's walking line is at 80% (`SCENE_FIT.ground`), so check it when one goes in.
