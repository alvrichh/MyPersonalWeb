# AGENTS.md — MyPersonalWeb

Guide for any AI agent (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI…) working in this repo from scratch. Read it first, then the files it points to. This repo is **public**: never add private or job-search data here (that lives in a separate private repo).

## What this is
Álvaro Rodríguez Molina's portfolio, deployed at https://alvrich.vercel.app. React + TypeScript + SCSS, built with Vite and **prerendered** so pages read without JavaScript. Eleven UI languages, light/dark themes, eight accent colours.

## Layout
- `src/components/` — header, mobile menu, pickers, icons.
- `src/sections/` — page sections (Hero, Experience, Education, Work, Skills, Contact, GitHub activity, Radar).
- `src/data/` — **content**: `experience.ts` (jobs and roles), `work.ts` (projects), `contact.ts`.
- `src/i18n/locales/*.json` — eleven translation files. Any user-visible text must be added to **all eleven** (`en` is the reference).
- `src/styles/partials/` — SCSS by section. `_mobile.scss` and `_responsive.scss` hold the phone layout (≤760px).
- `game.html` + `src/game/` — **Bug Hunter**, an Archero-style canvas game with Survivor.io-style weapons (move to dodge, stand still and ranged weapons fire; melee and magic work all the time; bugs drop XP gems; twelve rooms: ten fights with bosses in rooms 6 and 12, plus two shrine rooms (5 and 11); each level up offers one melee, one magic and one ranged or upgrade pick). The Spirits skill is a companion: one ghost per level floats at the hero's side (`SPIRIT_SLOTS`) and, while you stand still, shoots a purple `orb` shot at the nearest bug. Eight worlds of twelve sprints (`WORLDS` in `world.ts`): world 1 is the plain backlog, worlds 2–8 are themed by element with their own colours, a hazard (lava, puddles, thorns, lightning, bouncing shots, fog, mixed) and tougher bugs; finishing a world unlocks the next on the world map (`mapScreen` in `main.ts`, progress in `save.ts`). The map is built from the owner's island artwork in `static/images/game/map/` (one WebP per world, plus a 16:10 preview each; `rayo` and `nucleo` are hue-shifted copies of `infierno` and `demonios`): `ISLAND_META` holds, per image, where the ring, name plate and badge sit so the translated overlays line up with the art. It has a main menu with a shop (`shop.ts`: coins earned per run buy items worn in three slots, head, charm, relic, and the **forge**, seven permanent upgrades bought level by level, `UPGRADES`/`upgradePrice`, kept in `save.upgrades` and folded into the run's loadout by `buildLoadout(equipped, upgrades)` whatever is worn: vitality, might, armour, swiftness, magnet, fortune, wisdom; the loadout's `hasteMult`, `magnet` and `armor` are read in `world.ts`), an enemy-look picker (original, demon, Greek arena, nightmare, random; drawn in `draw.ts`), and sound options; the dialogs, HUD, cards and crests are cut from the owner's UI sheets into `static/images/game/ui/` (the two stone-and-neon frames, squat and tall, are drawn whole behind each dialog, never sliced: `fitPanel()` in `main.ts` measures the content, picks the frame whose shape is closer and sets height and padding from the bands in `FRAMES`, so the content sits in the plain centre and long dialogs scroll inside; the frame pictures are padded so the gem's axis is the exact centre of the file, so never recentre them with a transform; the card WebPs are cut without the dark matte the sheets carry around each piece, so no CSS masks are needed; every UI picture's visual centre is within 1px of its file's centre (measure the alpha centroid and the mid-row extent before adding one; `btn2_green` was rebuilt by mirroring its intact half because the sheet cut its left end); row cards place the text column exactly on the picture's body (ring 5–29%, body 29–91% of the width: columns `31.87% 1fr` with `padding-right: 9%`, since grid percentages resolve against the content box) and the icon on the ring's centre at 17%; side-by-side button rows are stacked everywhere because two bevelled bars are wider than the frame's plain centre and would shift the whole column; the owner's three chamfered bars as `border-image` (`ui/btn2_{blue,green,orange}.webp`: blue for the main action, green to play, orange for anything that costs coins; grey `btn_gray` for secondary buttons); crests for pause, level-up, shrine, victory, defeat and boss with the title set underneath; tall cards with a ring, title plate and bottom plate for the level-up picks on wide screens and row cards on phones, the same rows for the enemy-look picker; see `.ui-head`, `.panel .skill`, `.panel .style` in `game.css`); the health (green, with its own heart), XP (purple) and boss (red, spiked) meters come from the owner's HUD sheet, each split into an empty frame and a fill (`ui/meter2_{hp,xp,boss}_{frame,fill}.webp`; the track's position inside each frame was measured on the picture and is hard-coded in `game.css`): the frame keeps its shape and the fill is clipped to `--p` by `setMeter()` in `main.ts`, never resized; in a boss fight no crest is shown, the name sits in the horned plate (`ui/boss_plate.webp`) over the bar just under the HUD, and `resize()` starts the room below the bar so it never covers the playfield; each skill has a painted medallion in `static/images/game/skills/<id>.webp` (`skillIcon()` in `main.ts`), shown in the level-up cards and the HUD weapon row (`double` doubles as the bow's icon); the main menu is the owner's cover mock-up used as is (`static/images/game/cover.jpg`, with its words wiped off the picture) and transparent buttons plus translatable text laid over the painted controls at fixed percentages in `menuScreen` (`.cover*` in `game.css`); the canvas title parade (`scene.ts`) still runs behind the other menu screens. While the cover and the map art load, `game.html` shows its own purple loading bar (`.loading`), which `main.ts` fills as each picture arrives and removes before opening the menu (never later than 6 s). Rooms 5 and 11 (`SHRINE_ROOMS`) are shrines: no enemies, the door open, and a statue in the middle that, when touched, offers half your health back or a random new skill (`shrinePick`; `shrineScreen` in `main.ts`); the altar is drawn from `ui/statue_on.webp` and `statue_off.webp`. Difficulty is tuned per fight number, not per room (`stageOf`), so the shrines don't make later fights harder. Clearing room 8 (`WHEEL_ROOM`) also opens a second, purple door in the top wall at `WHEEL_X`, once per run: it leads to the wheel of fate (`WHEEL`, `applyWheel`, `leaveWheel` in `world.ts`; `wheelScreen` in `main.ts`), eight slices with four good, three bad and one empty, drawn on the owner's painted disc and pointer (`ui/wheel_disc.webp`, `wheel_pin.webp`), then on to room 9. Dying with at least 1000 coins (banked plus this run's, `REVIVE_PRICE`) offers, once per run, to buy your way back up with half your health (`paidRevive` in `world.ts`, `reviveScreen` in `main.ts`; the run's coins are spent first, then the bank). On localhost `window.bugHunter` exposes the running game so those screens can be reached by a script. The hero is drawn from pose pictures cut from the owner's character sheet (`static/images/game/hero/<skin>-<hat>-<pose>.webp`: two skins, boy and robot, picked in the Enemies/Styles screen and kept as `save.skin`; four head states, base or the worn head item, since the hats are painted on; five poses, idle, walk, back, shoot, cheer; `drawPlayer` in `draw.ts` picks one from movement, facing and whether the room is clear, flips it to face left and alternates idle/walk for a step cycle); the collision circle in `world.ts` is unchanged. Each world has its own cast of enemies (`look` in `WORLDS`: original bugs, demons, vikings, jungle, Greek arena, robots, nightmare; world 8 mixes them), drawn in `draw.ts`; the picker can force one. Every cast has painted sprites (`static/images/game/enemies/<look>-<kind>-<frame>.webp`; `drawEnemySprite` in `draw.ts`, `SPRITE_FRAMES`): original, demon and Greek have four walking frames; viking, jungle, robot and nightmare have six (three walking, two attacking shown while a boss aims/charges or just after a ticket throws, one taking a hit). The retro look uses the hand-drawn figures. Sheets are cut with a vertical margin and per-cell connected components so heads above the grid line survive and neighbours' halos don't. All audio is synthesised in `audio.ts` with the Web Audio API (drum and bass at 174 BPM: a 64-bar set where two "DJs" alternate, with a trance breakdown and a lifted second progression); there are no audio files. Progress lives in one localStorage key (`save.ts`). Balance changes are best checked by simulating games in Node against `world.ts`. Rules in `world.ts` (no DOM, so it can be simulated in Node), drawing in `draw.ts`, screens and input in `main.ts`, text in `strings.ts` (English and Spanish only). It reads the site's saved `lang` and `accent`. Options has a **Retro look** switch (`save.retro`, `data-retro` on `<html>`): the override block at the end of `game.css` turns the painted textures off (plain panels, flat buttons, bordered cards, emoji icons, one-row HUD) and `draw.ts` draws the ring-and-avatar hero and the hand-drawn statue instead of the sprites; `fitPanel()` steps aside in retro. The game's text is set in Fredoka (Google Fonts, loaded only by `game.html`; `--font` in `game.css`); the monospace numbers stay as they are.
- `static/data/experience.json` — a second copy of the experience data used by the legacy calendar page (`src/legacy/calendar`). **Keep it in sync** with `src/data/experience.ts`.
- `static/cv/alvaro-molina-cv.pdf` — the CV served by the "Download CV" button. Generated from `scripts/cv/cv.html` (see below).
- `scripts/build/` — build pipeline (prerender, GitHub contributions, sitemaps).
- `scripts/cv/` — CV source (`cv.html` + `assets/`) and `build.mjs` to render the PDF.

## Commands
- `npm install`, then `npm run dev` (Vite dev server).
- `npm run typecheck` — TypeScript check. **Run before committing.**
- `npm run build` — full build with prerender. Also rewrites `static/data/github-contributions.json` from the GitHub API; if you did not mean to change it, `git checkout -- static/data/github-contributions.json` before committing.
- There is no test suite (`npm test` is a placeholder). Verify UI changes in a browser: serve with `npx vite preview --port 4179`, then take screenshots with Playwright. In this environment use the installed headless shell `/opt/pw-browsers/chromium_headless_shell-1194/chrome-linux/headless_shell` as `executablePath`; do **not** run `playwright install`.

## Working rules
- **Mobile first check.** Anything visual must be checked at 360–390px wide as well as desktop.
- **Edit content in data files, not in components**, when the change is a fact (dates, titles, projects). Components only render.
- **Dates:** `YYYY-MM` strings. Keep them consistent with the CV (see the CV section below).
- **Git:** commit as the configured user; **no `Co-Authored-By` or AI attribution lines** unless the owner asks. Push to `main` only when asked. Pull (`--ff-only`) before you work; never force-push `main`.
- Keep commits small and in English conventional style (`feat(experience): …`, `fix(mobile): …`).

## The CV (`scripts/cv/`)
- The CV is an HTML template (`cv.html`) that reproduces the original Canva layout in Open Sans, A4, two pages. Edit text there, then `node scripts/cv/build.mjs` (needs Playwright; set `CHROMIUM_PATH` if the default browser is missing).
- Keep the CV and the website in agreement: same titles, same dates, same company names. The website's `src/data/experience.ts` is the reference for the public record; after changing it, update the CV too.
- Check the result visually: two pages, A4, no text overflowing a column, bottom margin roughly even on both pages.

## Portrait easter egg
- A click on the hero portrait spins it like a coin and sets off the current accent's element: blue water ripples, green leaves blown left, red flames round the ring, orange fireworks (streaks that fall as dots), yellow lightning, white ghosts, pink hearts orbiting, and confetti under Disco. Quick repeat clicks build a combo (more pieces, further).
- Code: `src/sections/HeroAbout.tsx` (click, combo, piece lifetime), `src/components/portraitFx.ts` (which effect per accent, where each piece starts and goes) and `src/styles/partials/_portrait-fx.scss` (shapes and animations). Keep the durations in `portraitFx.ts` (`LIFE`) in step with the SCSS.
- It is hidden on purpose: default cursor, no button role. The hints are one line in `src/i18n/bubble.json` (desktop, after the CV line) and a single wiggle of the portrait on touch screens if nobody taps it within 4 s of loading the page or of changing the accent.
- Rules it must keep: nothing runs with `prefers-reduced-motion`; on phones (≤760px) no piece may leave the screen sideways (check `document.documentElement.scrollWidth` while it plays); every piece is removed after its burst.

## Things that caused bugs before
- Menus and pickers on phones must fit inside the screen (they were once offset to the right).
- Background decoration in the CV must not overlap text; the bottom decoration is mirrored on purpose.
- Adding a new `.xp-…` element without the matching SCSS leaves it unstyled; check the section after every change.
