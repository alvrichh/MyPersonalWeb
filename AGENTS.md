# AGENTS.md — MyPersonalWeb

Guide for any AI agent (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI…) working in this repo from scratch. Read it first, then the files it points to. This repo is **public**: never add private or job-search data here (that lives in a separate private repo).

## What this is
Álvaro Rodríguez Molina's portfolio, deployed at https://alvrich.vercel.app. React + TypeScript + SCSS, built with Vite and **prerendered** so pages read without JavaScript. Eleven UI languages, light/dark themes, eight accent colours.

## Layout
- `src/components/` — header, mobile menu, pickers, icons.
- `src/sections/` — page sections (Hero, Experience, Education, Work, Skills, Contact, GitHub activity, Radar).
- `src/data/` — **content**: `experience.ts` (jobs and roles), `work.ts` (projects), `contact.ts`.
- `src/i18n/locales/*.json` — eleven translation files. Any user-visible text must be added to **all eleven** (`en` is the reference).
- `src/styles/partials/` — SCSS by section. `_mobile.scss` and `_responsive.scss` hold the phone layout (≤760px).
- `game.html` + `src/game/` — **Bug Hunter**, an Archero-style canvas game with Survivor.io-style weapons (move to dodge, stand still and ranged weapons fire; melee and magic work all the time; bugs drop XP gems; twelve rooms: ten fights with bosses in rooms 6 and 12, plus two shrine rooms (5 and 11); each level up offers one melee, one magic and one ranged or upgrade pick). It has two modes (`Mode` in `world.ts`, `save.mode`, picked on the cover's pill under the play bar or on the map screen's `.mode-pick`): **Sprints** is that room game; **On call** («Guardia», `'horde'`) is the Survivor.io take: `newGame(…, 'horde')` calls `startField()` instead of `enterRoom()`, the hero starts at (0, 0) on an open field with no walls (`keepInRoom` only clamps in sprints, `outside()` is never true there, shots and orbs die by their `life`; Ricochet arrows turn towards the nearest bug not yet hit, in both modes, so the upgrade still means something there), the camera rides on the hero (`draw()` centres the transform on `view.cx/cy`, which `resize()` sets to the middle of the area under the HUD; `drawField()` fills it with the plain middle of the world's floor picture, `FLOOR_CROP`, mirrored, as one repeating pattern baked once per floor (`fieldPattern`: a 2×2 block of the crop and its mirrors; drawing the tiles one by one left hairline seams at fractional camera positions); the retro grid covers `vis`, the visible rect) and `updateHorde()` runs the ten minutes (`HORDE_LENGTH`): spawns on a ring just past the visible edge (`ringSpot`, from `g.viewHalf`, which the page sets every frame from the canvas), faster and in bigger groups with the clock, tickets from 1:30 and legacy from 2:30 with a cap on tickets and on everyone (`cap`), toughness and speed scaling with the minute instead of the room (health ×(1 + 0.2 a minute) and ×(1 + 0.3 a world past the first; it was 0.45 a world, which the owner found too tough), and each world past the first pouring faster (7% per world), in bigger groups, with a higher cap and more frequent hazard patches, stragglers far behind teleported back to the ring, hazard patches (lava, puddles, thorns) dropped near you now and then, a coin bonus and a `'minute'` event each minute, bosses summoned at `HORDE_BOSS_AT` (5:00 and 10:00; boss HP there is its own, `bossHp`: a set 2520 and 7560 times the world's factor, what 8% a minute on 1800 and 4200 used to give at those minutes, the owner finding the clock-based health confusing; only Nightmare's bosses still grow 8% a minute; the second is `final` for its attacks; backups only while there is room), and the win when the last boss dies. There tougher bugs drop health hearts (`g.hearts`, `HEART_CHANCE`: tickets 6%, legacy 35%, bosses always three; each heals `HEART_HEAL` = 12% of max health, is drawn in when you pass within 60, and fades after `HEART_LIFE` seconds, blinking at the end; `drawHearts` in `draw.ts` draws the owner's `fx/heart.webp`, shifted from red to pink at his request). There, ranged weapons also fire while moving at 1/1.8 of the rate (standing still is still better), lightning strikes come slower, and levels start at 45 XP and climb geometrically (`xpNext × 1.2 + 12`) because XP pours in: about level 4 after the first minute, 11 at five and 19 at ten. Later worlds pay +50% each in sprints but +25% on call (`worldPay`), where they also pour in far more bugs: at +50% a world-7 shift paid about 10000 coins, more than a skin, and three times a sprint's coins a minute. Kills there pay 40% of a sprint kill (`HORDE_PAY`), because a won shift in world 1 paid about 3500 coins, half the shop in one go; now it is about 1400, so the forge takes about seven shifts and a skin about seven. Records per world in `save.hordeBest` (time, kills, won). **Nightmare** («Pesadilla», `g.endless`, `newGame`'s fifth argument, `NIGHTMARE_WORLD` = 8): On call in El Núcleo with no clock, started from the red «☠️ Pesadilla ∞» button the map shows under Play when world 8 is picked and open in On call (`play-nightmare` in `main.ts`): no win, a boss every `NIGHTMARE_BOSS_EVERY` (5 min), the cast's and the Chimera in turn (`nextBossAt`, `finalBossNow` in `world.ts`), every boss drops its chest, and the bugs keep coming faster without a top: the cap rises 5 a minute up to 320 and spawns quicken past ten minutes down to every 0.12 s, on top of the toughness that already climbs with the clock; it opens nothing, and its record is the longest run, `save.nightmareBest` (time and kills, wiped by a reset), shown on the map and the end screen («¡Nuevo récord de pesadilla!»). Each mode has its own world progress: sprints use `save.unlocked`/`save.world`, on call `save.hordeUnlocked`/`save.hordeWorld` (`unlockedIn()`, `worldIn()`, `setWorld()` in `main.ts`; the cover and the map show the current mode's), and winning a world unlocks the next only in that mode; `loadSave()` rebuilds both counts from each mode's own records (a sprint world won in `save.bestByWorld`, a shift won in `save.hordeBest`), because before the split, or on a page cached from then, an on-call win also opened sprint worlds. Balance was checked with a kiting bot (`scratchpad` sims): a careless bot lasts about three minutes in world 1, so a person should get much further; tune `updateHorde` if the owner finds it too easy or too hard. ## Working rules
- **Mobile first check.** Anything visual must be checked at 360–390px wide as well as desktop.
- **Edit content in data files, not in components**, when the change is a fact (dates, titles, projects). Components only render.
- **Dates:** `YYYY-MM` strings. Keep them consistent with the CV (see the CV section below).
- **Git:** commit as the configured user; **no `Co-Authored-By` or AI attribution lines** unless the owner asks. Push to `main` only when asked. Pull (`--ff-only`) before you work; never force-push `main`.
- Keep commits small and in English conventional style (`feat(experience): …`, `fix(mobile): …`).

- The CV is an HTML template (`cv.html`) that reproduces the original Canva layout in Open Sans, A4, two pages. Edit text there, then `node scripts/cv/build.mjs` (needs Playwright; set `CHROMIUM_PATH` if the default browser is missing).

- Code: `src/sections/HeroAbout.tsx` (click, combo, piece lifetime), `src/components/portraitFx.ts` (which effect per accent, where each piece starts and goes) and `src/styles/partials/_portrait-fx.scss` (shapes and animations). Keep the durations in `portraitFx.ts` (`LIFE`) in step with the SCSS.
- It is hidden on purpose: default cursor, no button role. The hints are one line in `src/i18n/bubble.json` (desktop, after the CV line) and a single wiggle of the portrait on touch screens if nobody taps it within 4 s of loading the page or of changing the accent.
- Rules it must keep: nothing runs with `prefers-reduced-motion`; on phones (≤760px) no piece may leave the screen sideways (check `document.documentElement.scrollWidth` while it plays); every piece is removed after its burst.

## Things that caused bugs before
- Menus and pickers on phones must fit inside the screen (they were once offset to the right).
- Background decoration in the CV must not overlap text; the bottom decoration is mirrored on purpose.
- Adding a new `.xp-…` element without the matching SCSS leaves it unstyled; check the section after every change.
